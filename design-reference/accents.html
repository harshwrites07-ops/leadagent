My recommendation

Lime × Coral, in that order — with cream as a typographic punctuation mark.

Single-accent systems are safe but generic. A two-accent system with strict semantic rules is the rarest thing in B2B SaaS and the most defensible. Lime owns the machine; Coral owns the human. The user instantly learns: green = my AI is working, coral = a real person needs me. Every other competitor has only one register; you have two.

1st choice · Recommended
Lime × Coral
Two accents with clear semantic ownership. Most defensible, most distinctive, most useful to the user.
2nd choice · Safer
Butter Cream
If "premium" matters more than "energetic." Editorial vibe. Quietly upmarket. Less ownable but unimpeachable.
3rd choice · Bold
Coral solo
If you want maximum warmth and humanity. Differentiates from every blue/green competitor. Slight error-color risk.
Avoid
Blue (Apollo, Outreach.io, Salesloft, Reply.io). Saturated green (Instantly). Saturated orange (HubSpot, Lemlist). Any color over 0.7 chroma will read as "AI bro" or "trading platform" — both wrong for the agency audience.
