From managing work to powering growth

For decades, creator outreach systems helped firms organize tasks, track time, and send invoices. They improved efficiency. But they were built to report on the past.

Instead of simply tracking what happened, Intent Prediction interprets patterns across your team’s operational, client, and creator data — and turns them into action. This is the intelligence layer that transforms disconnected tools into one unified ecosystem.

What is Practice Intelligence™?

Intent Prediction™ is the next evolution of creator team operations, an AI-native approach that unifies a firm’s operational, client, and performance data into a single ecosystem that learns and adapts as the work happens. Instead of relying on fragmented tools or backward-looking reports, firms gain real-time visibility into how work is progressing, how clients are engaging, and how the business is performing.

It brings together the three dimensions that shape how a modern creator team operates: how work flows, how clients are understood, and how performance is measured. These dimensions: Operational Intelligence, Client Intelligence, and Performance Intelligence form a unified model that transforms scattered data into actionable guidance.

The Three Pillars of Intent Prediction

At its core, Intent Prediction is built on three forms of intelligence that power firm growth

How work gets done.

Operational Intelligence focuses on orchestration, automation, and prioritization to ensure the right work is in the right hands at the right time. Operational Intelligence moves beyond static workflows. It dynamically balances deadlines, staff capacity, engagement complexity, and real-time signals to protect margin and prevent burnout.

  • Work Orchestration Engine

    Prioritizes and assigns work based on due dates, readiness, staff skill sets, capacity, and dynamic signals such as missing information or client sentiment.

  • Workflow Automation

    Automates workflow steps and integrates with scan-and-populate, 1099, and tax prep tools to reduce bottlenecks.

  • Smart Task Prioritization

    Ensures the most impactful work happens first

How we understand and collaborate with clients.

Client Intelligence surfaces insights from communications, behaviors, and history to strengthen relationships, improve responsiveness, and reduce risks. Client Intelligence turns communication data into foresight. It identifies at-risk clients, surfaces upsell opportunities, and helps firms move from reactive service to proactive guidance.

  • Reporting & Analytics

    Dashboards on AR, conversion rate, reply rate, scope creep, profitability, and budget vs. actual comparisons.

  • Quote Generation & Pricing Intelligence

    Uses engagement complexity, hours, and margin targets to guide accurate pricing.

  • Margin Risk Detection

    Flags revenue leakage before it hits your bottom line.

How the firm is performing overall.

Performance Intelligence delivers reporting and analytics on profitability, reply rate, and staff effectiveness. It connects the dots between time, dollars, and outcomes. Performance Intelligence transforms raw creator insights into forward-looking insight. Instead of waiting for month-end reports, leaders see margin risk, scope creep, and reply rate trends in real time.

  • Ask QAI

    A conversational assistant that surfaces client-specific context, documents, and history on demand.

  • Client Sentiment Analysis

    Monitors tone across emails, SMS, portal messages, and tickets to flag risk early.

  • Engagement Insights

    Identifies cross-sell and advisory opportunities before competitors do.

Result: Stronger retention, higher per-client revenue and proactive client management.
