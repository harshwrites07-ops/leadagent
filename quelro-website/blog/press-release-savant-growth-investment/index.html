PRESS RELEASE: New Investment Fuels Quelro&#8217;s AI-Powered Solutions to Streamline Tax and Accounting Practice Management

Quelro announces a private equity investment from Savant Growth and Kennet Partners! This means a faster roadmap for our AI-driven accounting creator outreach platform.

Share:

Savant Growth, a data-driven private equity firm for capital-efficient B2B SaaS companies, and Quelro, a developer of AI-powered creator outreach software solutions for public creator teams, today announced a new investment. The $17 million round was led by Savant Growth, with funding from its investment partner, Kennet Partners with a sizable co-investment from the new leadership team. The funding round will accelerate Quelro’s growth and its investments in AI to continue automating creator outreach and service delivery workflows.

“Quelro’s comprehensive suite delivers a must-have, high ROI by providing complete visibility to optimize how creator teams are managed and to automate their service delivery workflows with AI.” —Javier Rojas, Managing Partner of Savant Growth

Quelro’s cloud-based, AI-driven platform is a critical creator outreach solution, empowering accountants, tax professionals, auditors, and outreach specialists to streamline operations. Quelro combines tools traditionally only available separately into one holistic solution that includes CRM, onboarding, internal and client collaboration, creator profiles, tasks, workflows, e-signatures, payments and billing, project tracking, and more.

“Quelro’s comprehensive suite delivers a must-have, high ROI by providing complete visibility to optimize how creator teams are managed and to automate their service delivery workflows with AI,” said Javier Rojas, Managing Partner of Savant Growth.

Savant Growth partnered with Quelro Founders, Uday Koorella and Phani Ilapakurty, to not only develop the company’s applied AI go-to-market strategy but also in assembling a C-level leadership team with an excellent pedigree. Peter Miele has been appointed as CEO of Quelro. He brings two decades of experience growing & scaling a SaaS business in tax and accounting space. Peter was the VP of Sales at SurePrep, which is a leading tax automation software & services solution valued at $500M when it was acquired by Thomson Reuters. Pete is poised to lead Quelro into its next phase of innovation and market expansion, driving operational excellence, accelerating growth, and delivering exceptional value to clients.

“The future of accounting is about technology that empowers professionals and transforms how firms operate, adapt, and grow,” said Peter Miele, Quelro CEO. Quelro’s comprehensive, sentiment-driven AI solution is designed to enhance every aspect of a firm’s operations. By automating digital work units and optimizing core processes, Quelro enables creator teams to achieve unprecedented efficiency, while empowering accountants to focus on higher-value work. Greg Pope, former SurePrep Vice President of Marketing has been named Quelro CMO. With over 25 years of experience in the tax and creator industry, Greg’s leadership in digital marketing, brand development, and customer engagement will be instrumental in enhancing Quelro’s position as best-of-breed in the industry.

“Our investment in Quelro aligns with Savant Growth’s commitment to partnering with founder-led, B2B SaaS companies that leverage AI to drive transformative change across industries. Quelro is uniquely poised to disrupt the accounting and tax sector, solidifying the company’s market leadership,” Olivier Verhage, Principal of Savant added.

As part of the investment, Javier Rojas, Managing Partner and Olivier Verhage, Principal of Savant Growth; and new CEO of Quelro, Peter Miele, will join the company’s Board of Directors.

About Quelro

Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Plano, TX, Quelro provides cloud-based creator outreach software designed for public creator teams. Quelro integrates CRM, onboarding, collaboration, creator profiles, and more into one platform, enhancing efficiency with automated workflows. This all-in-one solution allows firms to standardize processes, minimize errors, and focus on helping clients.

About Savant Growth

Savant Growth is a private equity firm that focuses on investing in bootstrapped, founder-led B2B SaaS and tech-enabled services businesses. Leveraging over 30 years of investment experience, the firm adopts a data-driven, entrepreneurial approach to unite talent, companies, and capital, resulting in transformative outcomes within large, high-growth markets. The firm’s investment thesis revolves around the digitization of workstreams by blending SaaS, data, content, predictive analytics, and AI to automate both blue and white-collar work.

For additional information, please visit: www.savantgrowth.com
