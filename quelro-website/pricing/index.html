FAQs

Can I upgrade my subscription?

Yes, you can upgrade your subscription tier at any time.

Do you offer discounts? 😁

Discounts may be available for new customers based on your team’s affiliations, contract terms, or selected package. Contact us to see if your team qualifies.

Do you offer free trials?

Quelro does not offer free trials at this time. Instead, we offer personalized demos designed around your team’s workflows, goals, and current systems so you can see the parts of Quelro that matter most to your outreach.

Is QAI an additional cost?

QAI capabilities are included in select Quelro packages. Some advanced QAI features may be offered as stand-alone options in the future to give firms more flexibility as their needs grow.

Are support & onboarding included?

Technical support is included for all customers at no additional charge. Onboarding is provided for a one-time fee based on the number of users and the scope of implementation.

Can I pay monthly or annually?

Yes. Quelro offers both monthly and annual billing options. Monthly pricing is $149/user/month for Intelligence, $124/user/month for Pro, and $84/user/month for Essentials.

Book a meeting to discuss your options

Once you complete the form, you’ll arrive at our calendar, where you can choose a time to meet with a Quelro Advisor for 15 minutes. The Advisor will ask some questions to learn more about your team’s goals and pair you with the correct product expert for a full consultation.
